# First test results

| Task | Efficiency metrics | Interaction friction metrics |
|---|---|---|
| **Register** | Success rate: 91% · Avg time: 106s · Avg clicks: 13 · Avg errors: 1 · Avg time to find button: — | Misclick rate: 8.8% · Avg mental blocks: 1 · Avg help asked: 0.78 · Avg happy path adherence: 86% |
| **Login** | Success rate: 91% · Avg time: 27s · Avg clicks: 5 · Avg errors: 0.3 | Misclick rate: 3.3% · Avg mental blocks: 0.27 · Avg help asked: 0.11 · Avg happy path adherence: 92% |
| **Enrollment** | Success rate: 73% · Avg time: 133s · Avg clicks: 18 · Avg errors: 1.5 | Misclick rate: 12.5% · Avg mental blocks: 1.91 · Avg help asked: 1.22 · Avg happy path adherence: 74% |
| **Status** | Success rate: 78% · Avg time: 34s · Avg clicks: 6 · Avg errors: 0.6 | Misclick rate: 8.4% · Avg mental blocks: 0.7 · Avg help asked: 0.38 · Avg happy path adherence: 80% |

From these results and what we observed during testing:

- The button for the first task was hard to find — participants expected it to be immediately visible.
- Some people lacked the patience to wait for the instructions and interacted with the app during the explanation.
- This lack of patience also affected subsequent tasks, as some participants rushed and ended up not knowing what to do, expecting guidance despite having instructions on screen.
- The completion rate on the fourth task appears high (80%) but that is only if one does not account for the participants who dropped out on task 3 and never reached task 4.
- On task 4 there was an outlier who misclicked and ended up on the wrong page and could not recover.
- Mental blocks and help requests show a strong correlation, suggesting that rather than participants pausing to think, they genuinely did not know what to do next.

The SUS survey results were:

(92.5 + 100 + 100) / 3 = **97.5 average**

Qualitative observations from the second test:

- Participants liked that the app did not require all personal data upfront to create an account.
- There were a couple of misclicks on the file upload area — the image and the clickable area appeared misaligned.
- A couple of errors occurred because participants were aware they were being tested; for example, intentionally typing fewer than 16 digits in the credit card field.

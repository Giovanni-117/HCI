### The Role of AI in UI/UX Development: A Software Engineering Perspective

> AI tools have proven to be quite efficient when it comes to generating functional user interfaces, since in a matter of minutes you can have a prototype with well-structured
> components and a visually acceptable design. However, because they rely so heavily on standardized design patterns, the results tend to look very similar to each other,
> which limits the originality of the final product. From my perspective as a student, I believe AI can be a very useful support tool in the early stages of development,
> especially for defining the visual architecture or reusing common components. That said, the real user experience depends on contextual factors that AI still cannot fully
> capture, such as the end user's profile, their specific needs, or the constraints of the environment where the system will be deployed. In that sense, I think the role
> of the developer and UX designer does not disappear, but rather evolves. It is no longer just about building interfaces from scratch, but about knowing when and how to
> intervene on what the AI generates, adjusting interaction logic, accessibility, and usability according to the project's context. AI can handle repetitive and predictable
> tasks, but user-oriented decision making remains the responsibility of the human team.
